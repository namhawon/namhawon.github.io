About

I am an M.A. student in psychology at Pusan National University, advised by Prof. Jaesik Lee, and expect to graduate in February 2028. I study how older adults of varying abilities and experiences interact with AI agents, and how these agents can support their everyday lives and psychological well-being. My goal is to take a human-centered approach to designing AI agents that older adults can trust as partners, improving their quality of life while respecting their needs and values.

My path began with a major in child development and family studies, continued through a double major in psychology and the Psychological Data Science Convergence Track, and now centers on human factors and smart aging.

Research

Breadth of AI service use · HAI ’26

What expands the breadth of AI service use among older adults?

Using the 2025 Digital Divide Survey in South Korea (N = 6,245), we examined psychological factors associated with the breadth of AI service use among adults aged 19–64 years (n = 4,965) and adults aged 65 years or older (n = 1,280). AI literacy was positively associated with breadth directly and indirectly through performance expectancy, and the indirect association was stronger at higher levels of technological self-efficacy.

Trust calibration · M.A. thesis (expected Feb 2028)

Transparency and failure type in driving automation systems

My thesis examines how drivers notice and respond when a driving automation system fails, and whether explaining the system’s actions helps drivers keep their trust in line with how well the system actually performs. The experiment runs in our lab’s driving simulator.

Data collection: Sep 2026 – Feb 2027

Looking ahead

I hope to extend my research in these directions:

  1. Trust calibration in driving automation: how older drivers’ trust changes when the system fails, and what helps keep it calibrated
  2. Explainable smart-home agents: how AI agents at home can explain their actions and errors to older adults
  3. Emotion and trust in social robots: how emotion shapes the way older adults build trust with social robots

I would love to connect and collaborate with researchers working on these questions.
